About Zone2560

What is Zone2560?

Is this a council website?

No, it isn't but Zone2560 helps you send your garbage reports directly to the local authorities and collection teams responsible for keeping Kawempe clean.

We also publish these reports online, so that residents in the community can see what's already been reported and track the cleanup progress.
