Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Zone2560 handles your information.

Last Updated: 19 November 2026

Zone2560 is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of individuals who use our website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you interact with our garbage reporting and waste management platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Zone2560, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide when submitting garbage reports, contacting us or using other website services.

This information may include:

  • Name and contact information, such as telephone number or email address, where requested.
  • Location details relating to reported garbage collection problems.
  • Descriptions of waste management issues.
  • Photographs uploaded as evidence of reported problems.
  • Information submitted through contact or feedback forms.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, where collected by the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Zone2560 may be used for the following purposes:

  • To receive and process garbage collection reports.
  • To identify areas experiencing waste management challenges.
  • To help monitor and follow up on submitted reports.
  • To communicate updates about reported problems where contact details are provided.
  • To improve waste management services and website functionality.
  • To prevent misuse of the reporting platform.

3. Sharing of Information

Zone2560 respects the confidentiality of users' personal information. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with authorised personnel or relevant waste management service providers when necessary to investigate, coordinate or address reported garbage collection issues.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable laws or lawful authorities.

4. Data Storage and Security

We aim to protect the information submitted through Zone2560 against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse.

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be maintained to protect personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Personal information will only be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required or authorised by applicable law.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted, destroyed or anonymised where appropriate.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where implemented to support website functionality, improve user experience and understand website usage.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users have rights concerning their personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Object to certain forms of personal data processing.
  • Raise concerns about how their personal information is handled.

8. Third-Party Websites

Zone2560 may contain links to external websites or services. These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Zone2560 is not intended to collect children's personal information without the appropriate consent of a parent, guardian or other legally authorised person, except where permitted by law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, data handling practices or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact the Zone2560 website administrators.

Visit our Contact Us page to submit your enquiry.

12. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy is intended to support responsible handling of personal information in accordance with the applicable laws of Uganda, including the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.
