Waste Management Handling Guidelines

Select an article below to learn more about proper waste management practices:

1. Sorting & Segregation

Separate wet organic food waste from dry plastics, glass, and metals at home before collection day to make recycling easier.

2. Reduce, Reuse & Recycle

Use reusable shopping bags instead of single-use polythene (kaveera) and sell clean plastic bottles to local recyclers.

3. Composting Organic Waste

Peelings and garden waste can be composted into nutrient-rich soil fertilizer instead of piling up in trenches.

4. Hazardous & Medical Waste

Never mix broken glass, batteries, or used medical syringes with household garbage. Seal them safely.

5. Using Community Skips

Place tied garbage bags inside the skip container rather than dumping waste on the ground beside it.
